The group (course-portal) gets no key at all — zero. Others get no key either — zero. ANSWER_3: 640, why not others, 400 only gives root a "peek" key and takes away root's write key too. The course-portal team still gets nothing, so the helper is still locked out. 755 gives the team AND every stranger in the building a "peek and run" key. That's more keys than anyone needs, and now strangers can see the note too. 777 gives EVERYONE full keys: peek, write, and run. Anyone could scribble on the note or mess with it. Way too much trust for one file. 640 is the smallest, safest fix: root keeps its full key, the course-portal team gets a "peek only" key, and strangers get nothing. Just enough, nothing extra.
